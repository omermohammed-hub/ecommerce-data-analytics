# Retail & E-Commerce Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This project is an independent retail and e-commerce analytics case study inspired by the business model of UK retailers such as Perfect2Trade.

The project simulates a retail business operating across multiple product categories and serving both B2C and B2B customers. The objective is to build an end-to-end analytics solution that demonstrates how raw operational data can be transformed into actionable business insights.

The dataset used in this project is independently generated for portfolio purposes and does not represent actual Perfect2Trade customer, sales, inventory or financial data.

The project will cover the complete analytics workflow, from data generation and automated ingestion through data cleaning, database management, SQL analysis and Power BI reporting.

## Business Objectives

The business wants to understand:

* How sales and revenue are performing over time
* Which product categories and products drive revenue and profit
* How B2B and B2C customers differ in purchasing behaviour
* Which customers and regions generate the most value
* Which products have strong or weak profit margins
* Which products are fast-moving or slow-moving
* Where inventory shortages or excess stock may occur
* Which products and categories have the highest return rates
* What business actions could improve revenue, profitability and operational efficiency

## Key Business Questions

The analysis will investigate questions such as:

1. What are the key revenue and profit trends?
2. Which product categories contribute most to revenue and profit?
3. Which products have the highest sales and profit margins?
4. How does B2B performance compare with B2C performance?
5. Which customer segments and regions have the highest value?
6. Which products are at risk of stock-outs?
7. Which products are slow-moving?
8. What are the main causes of product returns?
9. Which categories have unusually high return rates?
10. What actions should management take based on the analysis?
